    Hirki Plus Patch (H.P.P.)

    Description

    Hirki Plus Patch (H.P.P.) is a modular gameplay and balance patch for Heroes of Might and Magic III on VCMI, designed for use with Horn of the Abyss.

    The patch focuses on making underused creatures, skills, spells, heroes, war machines, town buildings, and map interactions more practical and interesting while preserving the classic feel of Heroes III. Its changes are intentionally modular, so players can enable the parts they want and keep the rest of their setup close to the original game.

    ## A Modular Patch for Heroes III

    Hirki Plus Patch expands selected systems rather than replacing the whole game. The main goal is to give more meaningful choices to the player: secondary skills should feel more rewarding to pick, creature abilities should better reflect the identity of their units, ranged combat should have a clearer economy, and narrow spells should gain more tactical value.

    H.P.P. is built to work alongside Horn of the Abyss on VCMI and uses the content, factions, creatures, artifacts, spells, and gameplay systems provided by HotA as its foundation.

    ## Main Changes

    ### Artifacts

    Reworked and expanded: Armageddon's Blade | Golden Goose | Cornucopia | Shackles of War | Diplomat's Cloak
     New artifacts: Boots of Reinforcement | Leather Armor of Swiftness

    ### Creatures

    Spellcasting: Armadillos | Efreets | Gunfighters | Priests | Zombies
     New abilities: Cerberus | Hydras

    ### Heroes

    Reworked: Melodia
     Added as a regular playable hero: Roland
    Starting armies adjusted: tier 3–6 creature specialists

    ### New Intelligence

     Skill expansion: Intelligence retains its existing maximum mana bonuses and additionally provides combat mana during battle.
    Creature support: selected allied creatures associated with magic increase the hero's available combat mana based on their stack size.
     Bonus stacking: support from different creature lines can be combined, but splitting the same creature into multiple stacks does not duplicate its bonus.

    ### New Luck

    System expansion: Luck retains its original bonuses, while the effects of positive and negative Luck are greatly expanded through optional modules.
     Creature abilities: Luck can unlock, improve, or weaken selected chance-based creature abilities.
    Spells and artifacts: Fortune, Misfortune, and Magic Mirror gain new Luck-based interactions, while the Pendant of Courage is adjusted.
     Adventure map: the Luck skill can improve selected rewards from adventure-map objects, creature banks, and the Pyramid.

    ### New Ranged

    System rework: ranged units, ammunition, war machines, skills, spells, and artifacts are connected into a more coherent ranged-combat system.
     Ammunition: shooters receive a new ammo baseline, while the Ammo Cart provides additional ammunition instead of unlimited shots.
    Skills and spells: Archery, Artillery, and Precision are expanded, including stronger cooperation between shooters, the Ballista, and the Cannon.
     Heroes and artifacts: Torosar, the Golden Bow, the Bow of the Sharpshooter, and the Ammo Cart are adjusted to support the new system.

    ### Objects

    Object expansion: selected adventure-map objects retain their original effects and gain thematic, free creature upgrades or transmutations.
     Creature upgrade objects: Cover of Darkness | Faerie Ring | Magic Spring | Mermaids | Pillar of Fire | Redwood Observatory | Sanctuary | Swan Pond | Trailblazer
    Creature-bank rewards: Spit rewards Hydras instead of gold while preserving its original guards and object behavior.

    ### Skills

     Reworked: Sorcery now increases the hero's Spell Power during combat.
    Expanded: Estates | First Aid | Leadership | Navigation | Mysticism | Scouting
     These expanded skills retain their existing effects and gain additional economic, defensive, movement, or information utility.

    ### Spells

    Reworked: Mirth | Sorrow | Slayer | Magic Mirror
     New spells: Acid Burst | Ice Storm

    ### Towns

    Elemental Ritual: selected Mage Guilds allow heroes to sacrifice complete Fire, Water, or Air creature sets in exchange for matching Tomes and Orbs; carrying a complete set also grants temporary spell access and mastery of the corresponding magic school.
     Resource Specialist Silos: rare-resource specialists can receive a weekly reward from visited Resource Silos.
    Gold Specialist Halls: gold-focused heroes can receive a weekly City Hall reward, with Lord Haart included as a unique Estates-themed exception.

    ## Compatibility and Localization

    Hirki Plus Patch (H.P.P.) requires     VCMI 1.7.4 or newer and depends on Horn of the Abyss*. The mod was developed and tested for the VCMI 1.7.x line.

    H.P.P. is built as a modular patch for HotA on VCMI, with individual submodules describing their own detailed mechanics, thresholds, values, and compatibility notes.

    The Polish localization is written to stay close to the style and terminology of the classic Polish Heroes III translation where possible.
